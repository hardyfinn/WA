# Patch ideas

1. **Three-voice drift.** PITCH 1/2/3 and GATE 1/2/3 into three voices. Shift+GEN on each track with CHAR around 0.7, then SPREAD at 0.3 on track 2. Keep what you like.
2. **Call and answer.** Track 1 plays a sparse phrase; track 2 ANSWER = Resolve from T1; track 3 ANSWER = Mirror from T1, transpose +2. Mute and unmute to build sections.
3. **Arc performance.** A slow LFO (60 s) into ARC 1 and ARC 2, inverted on one, so one track grows while the other thins.
4. **Drums lead the melody.** A drum loop into LATTICE-X AUDIO; set track 1's Note lane clock to ONS. The melody steps with the drums while the rhythm stays on the grid.
5. **Morph set.** KEEP 4–6 variations of one track, MORPH on, and move X/Y with a slow random source such as VCV Random.
